How We Work

Three stages from brief to Day One.

A direct, transparent search cadence built to protect your engineering and leadership time.

  1. 01

    Role Calibration

    A working session to define what success looks like at 6 and 12 months: team context, technical bar, compensation band, and non-negotiables.

    Deliverable: Shared search scorecard
  2. 02

    Direct Outreach & Vetting

    Every candidate is interviewed personally for problem-solving depth, compensation fit, and genuine intent before reaching your calendar.

    Deliverable: Curated shortlist in 5–7 days
  3. 03

    Offer & Onboarding

    Structured offer calibration, notice-period check-ins, counter-offer navigation, and follow-through until your hire starts.

    Deliverable: Accepted offer & smooth joining
