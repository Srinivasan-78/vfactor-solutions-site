The Collaborative Journey

Five clear milestones. Complete transparency.

A structured, collaborative hiring process designed to save your team valuable time, eliminate guesswork, and ensure every candidate is genuinely excited to join your mission.

  1. 01

    Deep Listening & Role Discovery

    A collaborative working session to understand the true impact of the role: team dynamics, technical culture, compensation bands, and the non-obvious qualities that make someone flourish.

    You get: A shared Success Blueprint
  2. 02

    Targeted Talent Discovery

    Proactive outreach across our established networks, professional communities, and referral ecosystems — reaching top-tier active professionals as well as sought-after passive talent.

    You get: A live, active talent pipeline
  3. 03

    Deep Human Screening & Validation

    Every profile is personally engaged in a thorough conversation to assess real problem-solving capabilities, values alignment, compensation expectations, notice periods, and genuine enthusiasm.

    You get: High-signal, pre-vetted profiles
  4. 04

    Curated Shortlist & Interview Flow

    A prioritized shortlist accompanied by qualitative notes and interview briefing notes, with end-to-end interview scheduling and seamless candidate coordination handled on your behalf.

    You get: Executive shortlist & briefing
  5. 05

    Offer Crafting & Welcoming Day One

    Collaborative offer facilitation, transparent expectation management, counter-offer navigation, and continuous support right up to the candidate's first day and beyond.

    You get: Confident acceptances & joining

Flexible Engagement

Partnership models tailored to your goals

Choose the model that best fits your hiring roadmap. Transparent terms, zero hidden fees, and complete dedication to your team's success.

Contingency

Success-Based Talent Discovery

Ideal for individual open positions or immediate hiring needs where you want immediate reach with zero upfront financial commitment. You invest only when your chosen candidate joins.

  • Best for urgent, one-off, or growing team roles
  • Fee structured as a percentage of annual CTC
  • Complete replacement guarantee protection included

Retained

Dedicated Strategic Search

Ideal for executive leadership, confidential searches, and highly specialized technical mandates requiring comprehensive market mapping and dedicated search hours.

  • Best for C-suite, VP, and confidential leadership roles
  • Staged fee milestones aligned with search progress
  • Comprehensive talent mapping & market intelligence reports

RPO

Your Embedded Talent Partner

Ideal for growing organizations scaling multiple teams simultaneously. We act as an agile extension of your internal talent acquisition function on a predictable monthly model.

  • Best for continuous, multi-role, or rapid volume hiring
  • Predictable flat monthly engagement fee
  • Integrated with your ATS, employer branding, and processes

Not sure which model suits your roadmap best? Share your hiring plan with us, and we will advise you on the most cost-effective and time-efficient path forward.

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about how we work? Here is everything you need to know about our recruitment partnerships and candidate journeys.

How quickly can we expect to see qualified candidate shortlists?

For standard engineering, technology, and business roles, we deliver your first curated, pre-screened shortlist within 5 to 7 business days following our intake alignment session.

Because every single candidate is spoken with and validated for technical depth, notice period, compensation expectations, and culture fit prior to submission, your hiring managers save significant time by interviewing high-signal talent.

What makes vFactor Solutions different from traditional recruitment agencies?

Unlike large volume-driven agencies where requirements get delegated to junior desks, vFactor is a boutique, principal-led practice. You partner directly with founder Vijay Purushothaman, bringing 35+ years of cross-industry wisdom to your hiring strategy.

We do not forward unvetted resumes. Every profile is personally screened, verified against your requirements, and supported with continuous candidate engagement until day one of joining.

Do you offer a candidate replacement guarantee?

Yes. All contingent and retained placement agreements come with a standard replacement guarantee. If a placed candidate departs within the guarantee period, we will conduct a prioritized replacement search at zero additional fee.

How does the RPO (Recruitment Process Outsourcing) model work?

Under our RPO model, vFactor acts as an embedded extension of your internal talent acquisition function on a predictable monthly retainer. We integrate directly into your ATS, utilize your employer branding, and manage everything from sourcing and screening to interview scheduling and offer management.

This model is ideal for high-growth companies that need to scale multiple teams rapidly without hiring full-time internal recruiters.

Can you help US and international organizations hire talent in India?

Yes, absolutely. Having spent 6+ years living and managing business operations in the United States and 16 years as Country Head of Business in India, Vijay Purushothaman possesses unique cross-border expertise. We understand US work culture, timezone dynamics, and compensation benchmarks, making it seamless for international companies to build offshore and remote tech hubs in India.

Is there any fee or charge for candidates applying through vFactor?

Never. Our services are 100% free for candidates and job seekers. We never charge candidates for applications, interviews, or career advice. Your resume is kept strictly confidential and shared only with companies you specifically approve.
